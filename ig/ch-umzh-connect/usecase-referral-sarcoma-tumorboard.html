Referral - Sarcoma Tumor Board

Referral - Sarcoma Tumor Board

The patient PetraMeier has been diagnosed with a histologically confirmed synovial sarcoma of the right knee. The treating practitioner refers the case to the tumor board for multidisciplinary review and therapy recommendations.

Example ServiceRequest

Overview

This use case demonstrates a referral to a tumor board for cancer care review. The referral includes:

  • Primary diagnosis: Synovial sarcoma of the right knee
  • Supporting information:
    • Gadolinium contrast allergy
    • CT scan of the right knee
    • PET scan for staging
